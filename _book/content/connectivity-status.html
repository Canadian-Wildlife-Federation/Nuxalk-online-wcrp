Current Connectivity Status

In the Atnarko River, Necleetsconnay River, and Bella Coola River watersheds, 750 km of Pacific salmon habitat are currently connected to the ocean and 39 km are disconnected from it. This means that 95% of the 788 km of total habitat is connected

Structure Count

In the Atnarko River, Necleetsconnay River, and Bella Coola River watersheds, 47 structures potentially disconnect Pacific salmon habitat. Of these, 0 are identified as barriers in need of rehabilitation (priority barriers), 0 are identified as barriers that do not warrant rehabilitation (non-actionable), and 34 require further field assessment.
